Walk into any shop and every plate looks the same until you see it on *your* car. This is the exact way we guide customers in the workshop — four quick questions, and your pick usually becomes obvious.

## 1. What does your car look like?

The plate should belong to the car, not fight it.

- **Black / dark cars, stealth builds** → the black bases go straight to the top: [Basic Black Euro](/catalogue/basic-black-euro/) or [Glossy Black Euro](/catalogue/glossy-black-euro/) for contrast with white/silver lettering, [Pearl Black](/catalogue/pearl-black/) if you want depth under sunlight, or [Carbon Weave](/catalogue/carbon-weave/) if the car already has carbon trim.
- **White / silver cars** → [Glossy White](/catalogue/glossy-white/), [Pearl White](/catalogue/pearl-white/) or [Metallic Silver](/catalogue/metallic-silver/) keep the look factory-clean; [Basic Flat White](/catalogue/basic-flat-white/) is the cleanest, most OEM-adjacent option we make.
- **Colour cars & EVs** → this is where [Rainbow Stars](/catalogue/rainbow-stars/) and [Frosted](/catalogue/frosted/) shine — they carry personality without shouting.
- **Classics & retro builds** → [Classic Retro](/catalogue/classic-retro/) is built for exactly this; see also our [plate history article](/articles/number-plate-history/) for the old-school schemes.

## 2. Gloss, satin or matte?

This is a sheen decision, and it changes the whole character of the same colour.

- **Glossy** — maximum contrast and that showroom wet look. Shows dust and swirl marks a bit easier.
- **Satin matte** — the sweet spot most customers land on: [Basic Satin Matte](/catalogue/basic-satin-matte/) is subtle, modern and hides daily wear.
- **Full matte** — the stealthy choice: [3D Matte on Matte](/catalogue/3d-matte-on-matte/) puts matte lettering on a matte base, zero glare in photos; [Rough Matte](/catalogue/rough-matte/) pushes the texture even further.

Because we do our own spraying with the MIPA Germany system, the sheen is dialable — we can dull a gloss or warm it back up with clearcoat. ## 3. Standard lettering or Euro font?

The Euro-style font (wider, squarer characters) reads more "European" and pairs beautifully with black bases. It must still sit within the official character dimensions — the same rule as any plate, which we cover fully in [Are 3D-printed plates legal in Singapore?](/articles/3d-plates-legal-singapore/). We print every set to that spec regardless of font.

## 4. Want it to be *yours*?

- Add a **3D logo** — brand emblem or your own design ([Custom 3D Logo](/catalogue/custom-3d-logo/), [Custom Logo](/catalogue/custom-logo/)).
- **Kerning work** — we can tighten or tune character spacing for an exact look; see the [kerning-adjusted example](/catalogue/kerning-adjusted/).
- **Acrylic case / frameless kit** — the [custom diecast acrylic case](/catalogue/custom-diecast-acrylic-case/) finishes the install.
- On a **bike or e-bike?** Two-wheeler sets have their own sizes — see [motorcycle plates](/catalogue/motorcycle-plates/) and [e-bike plates](/catalogue/e-bike-plates/).
