Walk into any shop and every plate looks the same until you see it on your car. At AlisonT Design, photos and videos don't do justice to the product compared to looking at it in real life!

Follow me — build your plate step by step.

## 1. Pick your base colour

**European, Hong Kong or Japan vibes?** You have two directions:

- **White reflective base with black lettering** at the front, **yellow reflective base with black lettering** at the rear ([3D Glossy Black Euro](/catalogue/glossy-black-euro/)).
- Or **black glossy base** with white, silver or grey lettering ([3D Glossy White](/catalogue/glossy-white/)), or **black satin matte base** with white, silver or grey lettering ([3D Matt on Matt Gallery](/catalogue/3d-matte-on-matte/)).

## 2. Pick your lettering type

Now that you have decided which base colour you like, you get to choose the type of lettering. At AlisonT Design we have (full comparison on the [Lettering Types](/catalogue/lettering-types/) page):

- **Standard Letterings — 3 facets (U shaped):** Standard modern, bold strong.
- **Classic Retro Letterings — 2 facets (V shaped):** [Classic Retro Gallery](/catalogue/classic-retro/). Futuristic vibes. Reflect better then Standard Letterings.

## 3. Pick your lettering colours

With the lettering type and base plate decided, we move on to the lettering colours and design.

**Black base**

- **White letterings:** [3D Glossy White](/catalogue/glossy-white/), [3D Pearl White](/catalogue/pearl-white/)
- **Silver letterings:** [3D Metallic Silver](/catalogue/metallic-silver/), [3D Vicrom](/catalogue/3d-vicrom/) (hyper silver)
- **Special letterings:** [3D Frosted](/catalogue/frosted/) (looks silverish from far). Clear lacquer comes by default.

**White / yellow base**

- **Black letterings:** [3D Glossy Black Euro](/catalogue/glossy-black-euro/), [3D Pearl Black](/catalogue/pearl-black/)
- **Design letterings:** [3D Rainbow Stars](/catalogue/rainbow-stars/), [3D Carbon Weave](/catalogue/carbon-weave/), [3D Rough Matt](/catalogue/rough-matte/). *Lacquer is NOT applicable to these designs.*

**Then choose the lacquer.** For Solid White, Solid Black, Metallic Silver and Pearl White/Black letterings, you decide whether the letter stays **naked**, gets **glossy lacquer**, or gets **matte lacquer**.

- **Glossy lacquer** works like a protective layer and amplifies the effect — a shiny, glossy finishing.
- **Matte lacquer** offers similar protection but creates a duller effect, reducing the pearl/metallic shimmer.

## 4. Glossy or matte?

This is a sheen decision, and it changes the whole character of the same colour.

- **Glossy** — maximum contrast and that showroom wet look. Shows dust and swirl marks a bit easier.
- **Satin matte** — the sweet spot most customers land on: [2D Satin Matt](/catalogue/basic-satin-matte/) is subtle, modern and hides daily wear.
