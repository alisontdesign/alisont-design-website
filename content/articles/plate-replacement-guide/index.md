A cracked plate on the expressway is more than cosmetic — an unreadable registration number is an offence, and on sealed schemes the replacement has strict steps and deadlines. Here is the official process.

## Why you can't ignore a damaged plate

You cannot keep or use a vehicle without displaying the correct registration number assigned to it, and on schemes where sealing applies you must seal by the stipulated deadline — otherwise you face a fine of up to $2,000 or imprisonment up to 6 months [4]. The plate itself must present the number fully: characters 70 mm high and easily distinguishable, nothing obscuring them [3].

## Standard cars (no seals): the simple route

For ordinary private cars there is no seal to worry about — you simply replace the physical plate with one that meets the same legal spec (flat rectangular plate, securely fastened, correct colours and character dimensions) [3]. Many owners upgrade to a fresh 3D-printed set at the same time they replace a faded plate, since the new set must match the same rules anyway [3]. ## Sealed schemes (ROPC, OPC, weekend, classic, vintage): the strict route

If your vehicle sits under one of the sealed schemes, the rules add real steps [9]:

- Replacement plates for these vehicles "must be sealed at an authorised inspection centre. Failure to do so is an offence under the Road Traffic Act" [9].
- Before replacing the registration number, send the vehicle to an LTA-authorised inspection centre to confirm the number plate seals are intact; the seal inspection result is valid for 7 calendar days and the application must be completed within those 7 days [8].
- After a successful replacement, change and reseal the new number plates at any LTA-authorised inspection centre within 3 calendar days [8].
- If you need a seal broken for repairs or a part swap, vehicle owners must seek prior approval from the LTA to break the number plate seal(s) and re-seal the number plate(s) [9].

## Scheme conversions: the 3-day clock

Converting schemes (for example normal car to ROPC)? "Upon successful conversion of the vehicle, you have 3 calendar days to: Change your vehicle registration number plates at a sign craft shop/workshop to reflect the new vehicle scheme" [10]. ROPC plates then get sealed at an inspection centre [10]. Plan your plate order *before* the approval date, not after — three days is tight.

## What to look for in a replacement plate

Whatever your route, the replacement must satisfy the same rules as the original: flat rectangular plate securely fastened, characters 70 mm high with 10 mm spacing, front and rear visible and unobstructed, rear plate illuminated at night [3].
