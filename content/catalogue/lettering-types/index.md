## THE TWO LETTERING TYPES

Every plate we make starts with one of two lettering types. They are printed differently, and they sit on the plate differently.

### Classic Retro (V)

- **2 facets, V shaped**
- Futuristic look
- Reflects better than Standard Letterings
- Our signature look — see [Classic Retro Gallery](/catalogue/classic-retro/)

### Standard Lettering (U)

- **3 facets, U shaped**
- Modern, bold and strong
- The clean, familiar profile most people expect

## Which one?

Both meet OneMotoring dimensional standards. Choose Classic Retro (V) if you want the reflection and the futuristic edge. Choose Standard Lettering (U) if you want bold and modern.
