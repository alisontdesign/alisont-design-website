02 /

What makes us different?

We don't buy in plates. We make them.

Every orders starts as a 3D model on our screens. We print our own lettering in-house. That means we can accommodate almost any dimension you want: sizing, ratios, angles, tapering. If you can sketch it, we can build it. That's why the possibilities are almost endless.

Our own spray booth, our own colours.

We do our own spraying in-house with the MIPA Germany paint and clearcoat system. Matte in any colour. Full gloss. Or somewhere in between — we dial the sheen exactly where you want it, dulling the gloss or bringing it back by mixing in more clearcoat. Want a finish that isn't on the menu? That's the point of owning the booth: the menu is ours to write. We can also cut any casted acrylic to your measurements, to your shape, to your spec.

Everything in-house means we QC everything in-house.

Because nothing is outsourced, every single plate passes through our hands and gets inspected before it leaves. Everything we make is handmade — and with handmade, slight imperfections are possible. That's exactly why our QC matters: we make sure we love how it looks before you take it home.

We don't just hand you the plate and wave goodbye.

We take pride in this craft, and we read every bit of feedback we get. If something's ever off — any issue, any worry — we're here to listen and put it right. We're not selling you a plate. We're looking after your car's number plate needs, before and after the handover.
